The concept

One platform. Three experiences.

Three connected applications, one shared backend — built like a movie or slot-booking platform, purpose-built for gaming centers.

👤

Customer App

Customers discover nearby gaming centers, explore available games, check prices, select a date and time slot, and book instantly.

🎮

Gaming Center App

Gaming center owners manage their center, games, pricing, availability, slots and incoming bookings from one application.

🛠️

Admin Panel

Platform administrators manage users, gaming centers, bookings, payments and overall platform operations.

How the platform works

From a gaming center listing a station to a customer walking in and playing — the entire idea in nine steps.

01

02
